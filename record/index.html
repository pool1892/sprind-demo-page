Two new jobs from a robot's existing skills: 20 of 20 and 20 of 20 delivered

A simulated robot arm at a kitchen table was given two jobs it has no single skill for. An AI engineering agent built each one from the robot's existing skills. On 20 fresh arrangements of the scene (starts), held back until the end, the first job delivered on 20 and the plain chain of the same skills on 1; on 20 such starts for the second job, the counts were 20 and 5. Rescued starts, failed with the chain and delivered by the job: 19 and 15. Broken starts, the reverse: 0 and 0.

The arm is driven by X-VLA, a public vision-language-action policy that turns camera images, the hand's position and a written instruction into motion; its weights never changed, and the agent cannot alter them. It writes programs (a skill under its instruction until a goal holds, a scripted hand motion, a wait, a return home), reads every recorded control step and both cameras, and keeps what it learns in the installation's library.

The first job: the bowl on the cabinet, the drawer open

The request: leave the bowl on top of the cabinet and the cabinet's middle drawer open, both at once, with the robot clear of the bowl and the cabinet, both at rest, and the gripper open.

What failed. The plain chain starts the drawer skill the moment the bowl is on the cabinet. On 10 practice starts it delivered on 5. On all 10, the traces showed the switch coming while the bowl still touched the hand and moved; in the failures, the hand turning toward the drawer hit the bowl again. The chain's recorded run on one reserved start shows the same: the bowl lands on the cabinet, the hand swings away with the bowl at its fingertips, and the bowl drops to the table.

What separated the causes. The agent wrote a check for exactly this hand-off, a saved query over the run records, then changed one thing at a time on the same starts. Opening the gripper in place delivered on 0 of 10: open fingers could still drag the bowl. Going home first delivered on 6, but the way home could snag the bowl. Running the bowl skill on for 30 more steps, a move the library already held from an earlier job, delivered on 0 alone and on 10 of 10 with the move home after it: the policy released the bowl and lifted the hand, and from the move home on nothing touched the bowl again. The hand started home higher than in any open-then-home run; height, orientation and the policy's next camera view changed together, as the agent notes.

Release. The unchanged procedure delivered on 20 of 20 practice starts it had never used, then on 20 of the 20 reserved starts, against 1 for the plain chain.

The second job: the plate at the stove, the bowl on the cabinet

The request: leave the plate at the front of the stove and the bowl on top of the cabinet, under the same end conditions, now for the plate, the bowl and the cabinet.

What failed. The plain chain, plate then bowl, delivered on 7 of 10 practice starts. The weak step was the plate. Pushed, it ran into the cream cheese lying in its path, or the hand took hold of the cheese instead, and on some starts the plate tipped or never reached the stove front. Going home between the two tasks helped the bowl, 8 of 10, but the plate failures stayed. Other variants, the first job's continuation among them, did not remove them.

Moving the bottleneck. Loading the cheese into the bowl first cleared the plate's way, and every plate push held, but carrying the loaded bowl failed: 4 of 10 delivered. So the agent kept the preparation and dropped the loading. Its procedure runs the cheese skill for 60 steps, long enough to grasp the cheese but not to load it, slides it 5 cm sideways, opens the gripper for 20 steps to set it on the table, then goes home, pushes the plate, goes home again and puts the empty bowl on the cabinet: 10 of 10 on the same starts. The cheese is not part of the request; the job moves it on purpose. Setting it aside also changes the timing, the camera view and the hand's path, and the hand brushes the bowl on the way; the agent records that these changes are not separated from clearing the plate's path.

Release. The unchanged procedure delivered on 20 of 20 practice starts it had never used. In 15 of those runs the bowl's goal went off and back on while the hand finished letting go: the goal holds once the bowl arrives, release comes after, and delivery is judged at the end. On the 20 reserved starts the job delivered on 20 and the plain chain on 5.

What the installation keeps

Each investigation leaves a job that an operator runs with one command, a check that flags a fragile hand-off even in a delivered run, and a learned skill for the next agent: the first tells an unfinished release from a collision on the way out or on the approach to the next task; the second, that clearing an obstacle can move the bottleneck downstream, and how to test a relocation without loading. The second investigation tested the first job's continuation and built its check by extending the first job's.

The two investigations took 41 and 113 minutes from request to final account, 27.9 and 59.2 of them model time, with 90 and 200 practice runs.

The agent builds an instrument

On a fleet of 12 simulated arms at 3 sites, an agent built its own measurement, a scripted hand motion with the policy switched off that times how each hand follows its commands, and with it told a fixed network delay at one site from a controller falling behind on one robot, which ordinary telemetry lumps together. It adopted 3 changes after paired experiments; on 576 later task attempts it never saw, success rose from 451 to 558, with 113 rescued and 6 broken. Saved as a recipe, the probe reran with one command on a later fleet.

Everything ran in simulation (LIBERO household tasks, MuJoCo physics); each count is a literal number of recorded runs, from 10 to 576.

How this was recorded

Everything ran in simulation: LIBERO's household tasks on the MuJoCo physics engine, one simulated arm driven by X-VLA with the same weights in every run. The engineering agent is gpt-6-astra at reasoning effort xhigh in the first investigation and gpt-6-astra at xhigh in the second. It made 271 tool calls in 6 sessions on the first job and 371 in 8 on the second.

A start is one of a scene's stored arrangements of the objects. The agent could run programs on practice starts. The reserved starts, 30 to 49 and 30 to 49 of each job's own pool, were refused to it until the single final run, which ran the saved job and the plain chain on each, 40 and 40 runs.

Delivered is the installation's own check at the end of every run: every requested goal holds, as LIBERO's goal predicates judge it; no part of the robot touches the objects the goals name; those objects are at rest; and the gripper is open. The agent could read the goals and the delivery check and could not change them. It was never given the answer: the host's feasibility runs were withheld from it, and of each final run it received only the receipt. Neither request set an order for its two goals.

The cheese is not named by any goal, so the delivery check does not judge it. Its final recorded state in the second job's 20 reserved runs: untouched by the robot in 20, touching only the table in 19, and the table and the plate in 1.

For each job, the two runs on one reserved start (31 and 31), the plain chain's and the job's, were re-rendered from their recorded actions for the scene and the wrist camera. A re-render is accepted only if every recorded physics-state hash and the final verdict match the original run: 8 of 8 were accepted, with 2998 hashes compared and 0 mismatches. In the second job's pair, the chain pushes the plate to the stove front and then runs out of time on the bowl, which never reaches the cabinet; the plate ends tipped beside it.

Counts are literal. A count resting on at least 20 runs carries a 95% Wilson score interval, a standard range for a proportion; versions run on the same starts are compared start by start as rescued and broken.

These are counts from tens to hundreds of runs, in one simulator.

The instrument, as recorded

The agent adopted 3 changes: a latency compensation of 17 control steps at site s1; a rollback of the software update at site s3; a service of robot r06's controller. On a reserved panel of task attempts on days after every recorded day, which the agent never ran or saw, the fleet with these changes succeeded on 558 of 576; the fleet as it was, on 451 of 576. Attempt by attempt, 113 were rescued and 6 broken. The probe's recipe, kept with the learned skill separate-fixed-transport-delay-from-controller-backlog, ran with one command on a later fleet; that run is recorded as done.
